About the project

Practical DevOps learning for people who want real context.

DevOps Hub is a structured learning site on wystone.tech that focuses on the tools people actually use: Docker first, then Kubernetes, CI/CD, cloud platforms, and Terraform. The goal is to explain not just commands, but the reasons behind them.

What you will find here

The site is organized so learners can move from beginner-friendly introductions to more advanced workflows without losing the thread. Each section aims to answer three questions: what the tool is, why teams use it, and how it fits into production systems.

Why it exists

Most documentation is either too shallow for real use or too dense for someone who is just getting started. This project is meant to bridge that gap with practical examples, clear explanations, and enough structure to make the material searchable and easy to return to later.

How it is funded

Like many independent learning resources, the site is supported by advertising so the content can remain free to access. That support helps cover hosting and maintenance while keeping the material available without a paywall.
