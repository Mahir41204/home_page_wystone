Privacy policy

Clear information about how the site handles data.

DevOps Hub keeps data collection deliberately light. The site is designed as a free learning resource, and this policy explains the limited ways information may be collected through advertising, analytics, and standard web hosting services.

Information we collect

We do not require user accounts or collect personal information through forms. We may receive basic usage information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, and pages visited through standard analytics or hosting logs.

Advertising and cookies

The site may use Google AdSense and related cookie technologies to serve ads and measure performance. Those services may set cookies or use similar identifiers according to their own policies. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings and any consent prompts shown on the site.

Third-party services

Some content and infrastructure may be delivered by third parties such as Google or a hosting provider. Those services may process technical data needed to keep the site available, secure, and functioning correctly.

Questions

If you have a privacy question or want to request a correction, please use the contact information provided on the site. This page may be updated as the project grows or if the way the site is hosted changes.
